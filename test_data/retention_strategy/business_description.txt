We are a major telecommunications provider offering phone and internet services to thousands of customers. Recently, we've noticed an alarming increase in customer churn (attrition), where customers terminate their contracts to move to competitors. Acquiring a new customer is significantly more expensive than retaining an existing one. Our retention department has a limited budget and can only target a subset of customers with special offers. Currently, their targeting is random or reactive (waiting for a customer to call to cancel). We need a proactive solution to identify customers at high risk of leaving before they make that decision.
