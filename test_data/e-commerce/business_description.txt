We are a UK-based online retailer specializing in unique all-occasion gifts. Our company has been operating since 2010, selling primarily to wholesalers and individual customers across Europe and globally. While our revenue has been growing, we lack a deep understanding of our customer base. We suspect that a significant portion of our revenue comes from a small group of high-value customers, but we haven't quantified this. Furthermore, we experience seasonal fluctuations that cause inventory issues—sometimes we overstock, and other times we run out of bestsellers. We want to transition from a 'gut-feeling' approach to data-driven decision-making to optimize our marketing spend and inventory management.
