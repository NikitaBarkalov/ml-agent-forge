Our investment firm, 'NextGen Real Estate', buys residential properties in bulk to renovate and resell. Our current valuation process is manual, slow, and prone to human bias, leading to bad investment decisions (overpaying for properties). We have historical data on house sales in the Ames area, which includes detailed information about the physical characteristics of the properties and their final sale prices. We want to automate the initial screening process by using an algorithm that estimates the fair market value of a house based on its features.
